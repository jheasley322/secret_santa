Help for kids

You got a text. Now what?

← All help

Keep it secret!

Your link is just for you. Don't share it. Don't show your screen.

Once the names are picked, your page will say who you drew. Don't tell anyone, not even your best friend. That is the whole fun of Secret Santa.

Your wish list page

Tap the link in your text. It opens your page.

  • Add things you would like, up to 10.
  • Add a link for any of them, if you have one.
  • Say your favorite color and snack, your sizes, and anything you would like people to avoid.
  • Tap I'm done! when your list is ready. That tells your Elf you are finished.
  • Look at everyone else's lists.

Who did I draw?

When your Elf sends the names, you get a text. Open the same link. Your page says You drew... and shows their name and their wish list. Shh! Keep it secret.

"Names were reshuffled" text

Sometimes your Elf picks the names again. You get a text that says the names were reshuffled and tells you your new person.

  • Your old name no longer counts. Do not buy for them.
  • Use the same link as before. It now shows your new name.
  • Keep the new name secret too.

Not sure which name is right? Open your link. The name on the page is always the right one. Or ask your Elf.

What if something goes wrong?

  • The text never came: ask your Elf to send it again.
  • The link says "link not found": it may have been copied wrong. Ask your Elf for a new copy.
  • Lost the text: ask your Elf. They can send the same link again.

When in doubt, ask your Elf.
